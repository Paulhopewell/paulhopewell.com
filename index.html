The Proof

We Have Walked This Path

Our founder, Paul Hopewell, started as an apprentice electrician in a Nottinghamshire mining village. He bought his father's intruder-alarm business when his father was diagnosed with Parkinson's — grew it to 2.5× its value, sold it, and paid his father in full.

He then built Synergy (PH) Ltd — a health-insurance business he started knowing nothing about the industry. He learned on the job, systemised every process, delegated the day-to-day, and scaled it to a multimillion-pound exit.

He is now building again — this time from Qatar, using AI as the accelerant. That is the playbook Paul Hopewell LLC exists to deliver to other owners.
